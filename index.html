    How does this work?

    Your browser reads the public follower and following lists of the handle through a free X mirror API, then compares the two. Nothing goes to a server we run. Each scan is saved in this browser only, so the next scan can show who left.

    Only public accounts work. Lists can come back a person or two short, and someone who deleted or was suspended looks the same as someone who unfollowed, so we check those and label them. Accounts over 5,000 followers are too slow to scan.

    Your saved data

    Scans are stored in this browser (up to 5 per handle). Clearing your browser data erases them, so export a backup if you care about the history.
