    How does this work?

    Drop a public handle and we line up who follows you against who you follow, so you can see who isn't on the same page. Every scan is saved in your browser only, never on a server, so the next run can show who dipped.

    Public accounts only. Lists can come back a person or two short, and someone who deleted or got suspended looks exactly like someone who unfollowed, so we tag those. Accounts over 5,000 followers are too slow to scan, sorry.
